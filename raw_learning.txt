6) Sementic Search:
	1) Euclidean: Measures the straight-line distance between two vectors in space.“How far apart are these two meanings?”
		How it works in semantic search:
			1)Treats embeddings as points in space.
			2)Smaller distance = more similar.
			3)Sensitive to magnitude (length) of vectors.
			
		When it’s useful:
			1)When vector scale matters.
			2)Less common for text embeddings unless vectors are normalized.
	


	2) Cosine Similarity: Measures the angle between two vectors (not their length). “Are these two meanings pointing in the same direction?”
		How it works in semantic search:
			1)Focuses on direction, not magnitude.
			2)Most common choice for text embeddings.
			
		When it’s useful:
			1)Comparing semantic meaning of text.
			2)When vector length shouldn’t affect similarity.
	
	
	3) Dot Product: Measures similarity by multiplying and summing vector components. “How much do these two meanings reinforce each other?”
		How it works in semantic search:
			1)Larger value = more similar.
			2)Combines direction + magnitude.
			3)If vectors are normalized, dot product = cosine similarity.
			
		When it’s useful:
			1)High-performance search systems.
			2)When embedding norms carry meaning.

8) Reciprocal Rank Fusion (Score Creating): If we have N list of Search ways:  RRF = 1/(k+ Rank in list 1) + 1/(k+ Rank in list2)  + 1/(k+ Rank in list 3) ............ + 1/(k+ Rank in list N)
	Now from Point 7 , List1 (keyword search) has 35 documents and List2(Similarity search) has 30 documents
	1) lets say document1 has Rank is List1 is 2 and in List2 is 1
		let k = 0
		score  for document1 = 1/(0+2) + 1/(0+1) = 1.5
		
	That's going to assign Score and ReRank All the Documents
		
	RRF : Doesn't Depend on Score of it Depend on Rank in Each List
	
	What Should be Value of k: if k = 0 , then Rank 1 score is (1) which is 10 times more score than Rank 10 (0.1), Rank 1 Dominate significantly
				if k =50 ,  Rank1 score is 1/50 and Rank10 is 1/60  which is 1.2 times, Single high rank Doesn't Dominate Overall Ranking
				
	Beta: Weighting Semantic vs keyword, for Beta = 0.8,  New Score : RRF Score of Semantic * 0.8  + RRF Score of Keyword Search *0.2, beta  = 0.7

9) Retrival Evaluation:  Is it Finding Relvant Documents and upto What Extent.
	What you will need to Evaluate
	1) Prompts
	2) Ranked Result Documents for the given Prompts
	3) Ground Truth what are the Actually Relevant Documents (if you want to Evaluate you retriever you need to know the correct Answers)
	
	PRECISION : No-of-Relevant Document Retrive by Retriever/ Total Retrieved : 
	1) Penalizing Retriever for Returning Irrelevant Results
	2) Want to Reducing False Positive
	
	Recall :  No-of-Relevant Document Retrive by Retriever/ No-of-Relevant Document Actually Present
	1) Penalizing Retriever for  leaving out relevant documents
	2) Want to Reduce False Negative
	
	
	Retrival Metrics are influenced by how many documents the retriever returns
	Metrics Are Discussed in Terms of TOP-K Documents
	Define as Precision@k , and Recall@k
	Example k =5
	Precision@5 = (total Relevant Document till to 5 documents)/ 5
	Recall@5 = (total Relevant Document till to 5 documents)/(Actual Number of Relevant Documents for the Given Query)
	
	MEAN AVERAGE PRECISION : MAP@K evaluates average precision for relevant Documents in first k documents
	average precision@k  = (precision@1 + precision@2 + precision@3 + .................. +precision@k) /(total Relevant Document Retrieve till to k documents)
	Now Calculating Average Precison@k for Many Prompts and take Mean of it. the you get your MAP (MEAN Average Precision)
	MAP: Rewards Ranking Relevant Document Highly.
	
	Reciprocal Rank : Measures the Rank of the first relevant documents in the Returned list. 
			 :1/ (Rank of first Relevant document in the list)
	Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR): Calculating Reciprocal Rank for Multiple Prompts and Take mean of it.	
	
	Summary:
	Recall or recall@k: Most Cited metric, captures fundamental goal of finding relevant documents
	Precision and MAP@K: Asses Irrelevant documents and Ranking effectiveness
	Mean Reciprocal Rank: How Well model Performs at very top of Ranking
	
	Metrics help:
	Evaluate retriever Performance.
	Check if adjustmenst improve Results.
